# Day 1 — 15 posts

Mix, not 15 ads. From a small account, 15 promotional posts a day suppresses
reach; 5 originals plus 10 targeted replies is what actually compounds.

## Originals (5) — spread across the day

**1. The demo — 9am. This is the one that matters.**

I made a thing where you paste any product link and get a full infomercial for it in 10 seconds.

Real product. Real photo. AI pitchman who will not stop yelling about it.

Costs me 6 cents to make.

> Attach a 30s screen recording: paste URL → product preview appears → ad plays. No talking, no intro. Let it land.

**2. The economics — 12pm**

An agency charges $200-2000 for a product video and takes a week.

Mine costs $0.06 and takes 10 seconds.

I'm not saying it's better. I'm saying at 3000x cheaper you can make 40 of them and keep the one that works.

**3. Build in public — 3pm**

Shipped today: paste a Shopify link, it scrapes the product photo, and that photo conditions the video so the pitchman holds YOUR actual product.

Not a stock lookalike. The real thing.

Hardest part wasn't the AI. It was not getting SSRF'd by my own feature.

**4. The ask — 6pm**

Looking for 10 Shopify/Etsy sellers who want a free product ad made.

You give me a product link. I send you an MP4 you can post anywhere.

No catch, I want to see what breaks.

Reply with a link.

**5. The honest one — 9pm**

Week 1 of my AI TV channel: 204 users.
Week 2: about 20 a day.

Turns out nobody wants to watch AI TV.

But a bunch of people asked if they could use it to make ads for their own products. So that's what I'm building now.

## Replies (10) — where the actual growth is

Search these and reply with something useful, not a pitch. Lead with help,
mention the tool only if it genuinely answers them.

| Search | What to say |
|---|---|
| `shopify ad creative` | What's converting for them right now; offer to make one free |
| `UGC ads expensive` | The cost math; offer a free one |
| `product video freelancer` | Ask what they paid and how long it took — genuine market research |
| `dropshipping creative testing` | Volume is the whole game; you can make 40 variants for $2.40 |
| `veo 3 OR sora ads` | Where those models fall down for product ads (they invent the product) |
| `AI video tools` | Specific: image-to-video conditioning is what makes product ads work |
| `etsy marketing` | Offer a free ad |
| `TikTok shop creative` | Ask what format converts; 9:16 question is real research |
| `@levelsio` posts | Genuine engagement, he built the adjacent thing |
| Anyone posting a new product | "Want me to make you an ad for this? free, takes 10s" |

That last row is the highest-value action on this list. Someone launching a
product today has maximum motivation and no creative. Do it 10 times a day.

## What to measure

Not likes. Track: product links replied to you, ads generated, files
downloaded. Wire the GA key events first — right now the funnel is invisible.

## Repeat daily

Rotate the 5 originals through: demo, economics, build-in-public, direct ask,
honest/behind-the-scenes. Keep the 10 replies constant. The demo video is the
only one that needs to be genuinely new each time — use a different, funnier
product every day.
